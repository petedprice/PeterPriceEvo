Evolutionary Genomics · Wright Lab, University of Sheffield

Studying the causes and consequences of sexual dimorphism.

I'm a postdoctoral researcher in the Wright Lab at the University of Sheffield, working on the genetic conflict and competition that shapes sexual traits — from sex-linked inversions in zebra finches to meiotic drive in stalk-eyed flies. My work combines population genomics, single-cell approaches, and long-read sequencing across the tree of life.

About

I did my PhD here too, on conflict and competition in the evolution of sexual traits, and before that an MSci in human genetics at UCL, working on computational methods for detecting positive selection. Along the way I've picked up fieldwork in Peninsula Malaysia, single-cell library prep, and a fair amount of time on the university's HPC cluster.
